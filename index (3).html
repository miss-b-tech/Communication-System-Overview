Meet the Systems

Engineers judge a communication system with four criteria. Learn them first, then tap each channel to see its benefits and drawbacks. Open all four channels before you answer the questions.

SpeedHow fast the message gets out to people.
ReachHow many people it gets to, and how far away.
FormatThe kind of content it can carry: text, images, audio, or video.
InteractivityWhether the audience can respond, ask, or share back.

Score the Systems

Scenario: The ice stormAn ice storm has knocked out power across town for three days, and many cell towers are down too. The town needs to tell every resident where the warming shelters are.

Rate how well each system would work in this situation, from 1 (terrible) to 5 (excellent). Don't copy the ratings from Part A. Ask yourself whether people could actually get the message right now. The totals add up on their own.

Hint: think about what each system needs in order to work.
